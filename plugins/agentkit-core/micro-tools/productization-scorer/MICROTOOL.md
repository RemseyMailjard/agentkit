# Productization Scorer
## Responsibility
Score whether repeated consulting/training work can become a reusable product or managed service.
## Factors
repeatability, demand, pain intensity, standardizable inputs, automation potential, support burden, differentiation, margin, sales simplicity, recurring value.
## Output
score 1-10; strongest evidence; blockers; recommended product form.
## Boundary
Do not perform broader analysis, rewriting, or decisions outside this responsibility; return findings for the parent Skill to act on.
