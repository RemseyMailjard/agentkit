# Impact Estimator

## Responsibility
Estimate likely blast radius of a proposed change.

Use engineering and consultancy principles.

## Assess
- behavior;
- interfaces/contracts;
- data;
- security;
- tests;
- deployment;
- documentation;
- dependencies;
- users/operations;
- training/adoption implications where relevant.

## Output
Confirmed impact, plausible impact, severity and validation needed.

## Boundary
Label uncertainty clearly.
