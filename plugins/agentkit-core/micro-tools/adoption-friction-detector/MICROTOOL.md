# Adoption Friction Detector
## Responsibility
Find reasons users may not adopt a new tool, process or AI capability.
## Check
perceived value, workflow fit, trust, skill gap, change fatigue, governance ambiguity, permissions, UX, support, manager reinforcement.
## Output
friction; audience; impact; intervention; evidence.
## Boundary
Do not perform broader analysis, rewriting, or decisions outside this responsibility; return findings for the parent Skill to act on.
