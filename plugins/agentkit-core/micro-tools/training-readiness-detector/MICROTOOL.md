# Training Readiness Detector
## Responsibility
Determine whether a training can realistically be delivered successfully.
## Check
content, labs, instructor prep, accounts, licenses, permissions, tooling, network/proxy, MFA, participant devices, timing, fallbacks.
## Output
READY / READY WITH RISKS / NOT READY; evidence; blockers; actions.
## Boundary
Never mark an environment verified unless it was actually checked.
