# Change Impact Checker

## Responsibility
Assess what a proposed change may affect.

## Check
- behavior;
- interfaces/contracts;
- data;
- security;
- tests;
- documentation;
- dependencies;
- users or operations.

## Output
- impacted areas;
- likely severity;
- required validation;
- rollback/reversibility note where relevant.

## Boundary
Separate confirmed impact from plausible impact.
