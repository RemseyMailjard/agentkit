# Strategic Reflection Principles

- Evaluate progress against direction, not busyness.
- Look for recurring patterns across client work, training, products and personal energy.
- Identify what to stop, continue, systematize or productize.
- Watch for idea proliferation and context switching.
- Distinguish strategic bets from operational obligations.
- Prefer a few compounding assets over many isolated efforts.
- End reflection with one strategic adjustment and one concrete next action.
