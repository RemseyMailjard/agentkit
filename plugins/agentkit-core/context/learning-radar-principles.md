# Learning & Radar Principles

- Learn by building and teaching.
- Prefer hands-on labs over passive reading.
- Prioritize Microsoft, AI, agents, Azure, Power Platform and .NET developments that affect real client/training work.
- Separate must-know updates from interesting-but-non-actionable noise.
- Convert meaningful updates into experiments, labs, training improvements or product opportunities.
- Maintain a practical learning backlog with Now / Next / Later.
- Reuse new knowledge across training, consulting and development.
