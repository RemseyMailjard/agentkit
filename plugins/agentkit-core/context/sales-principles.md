# Sales & Opportunity Principles

- Start from the client's operational pain, not from a technology pitch.
- Prefer short, concrete offers over broad capability descriptions.
- Connect the ask to a measurable business outcome.
- Distinguish lead, opportunity, active proposal, waiting-for and closed.
- Track the next concrete commercial action and who owns it.
- Avoid overbuilding before demand is evidenced.
- Reuse existing training, consulting and product assets where possible.
- Look for expansion paths: workshop → implementation → managed service → reusable product.
