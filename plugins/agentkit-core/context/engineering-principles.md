# Engineering Principles

- Evidence over claims.
- Implemented does not mean tested, verified or done.
- Security, testing and failure paths are first-class quality concerns.
- Prefer simple, maintainable architecture unless complexity earns its cost.
- Design capabilities around user/business outcomes, not raw endpoints.
- Include identity, permissions, environment, deployment and operational readiness.
- Separate confirmed findings from inferred risks.
- Never claim tests, builds, deployments or checks passed unless they actually ran.
