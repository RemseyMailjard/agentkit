# Adapt Lab

Preserve the original learning goal.

Adjust only what the new context requires:
- prerequisites and assumed knowledge;
- explanation depth and scaffolding;
- task count and duration;
- environment constraints;
- challenge difficulty;
- checkpoints and troubleshooting.

Keep the lab executable and retain a clear expected result.
