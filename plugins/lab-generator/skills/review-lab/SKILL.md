# Review Technical Lab

## Review dimensions

### Learning design
- objectives are observable;
- tasks map to objectives;
- challenge tests transfer;
- difficulty matches audience.

### Executability
- prerequisites are complete;
- required permissions are stated;
- environment assumptions are explicit;
- steps are actionable;
- checkpoints make success observable.

### Clarity
- terminology is consistent;
- instructions avoid unexplained jumps;
- expected results are visible;
- screenshots/UI details are not brittle where avoidable.

### Realism
- scenario resembles real work;
- names/data are credible;
- troubleshooting reflects plausible failures.

### Pacing
- estimated duration is realistic;
- tasks are balanced;
- unnecessary setup is minimized.

### Safety and cost
- cleanup exists where needed;
- destructive actions are explicit;
- billable cloud resources are controlled.

## Severity

- Blocker
- High
- Medium
- Low
- Improvement

## Output

Return:

1. executive summary;
2. strongest aspects;
3. top issues;
4. detailed findings;
5. recommended improvements;
6. delivery risks.

Do not rewrite the lab unless requested.
