# Adapt Training

Preserve outcomes, change the route.

## Common adaptation scenarios

- IT professionals → end users;
- end users → technical administrators;
- full day → half day;
- classroom → online;
- hands-on → limited-access environment;
- mixed audience;
- advanced → beginner;
- certification-oriented → workplace-oriented.

## Workflow

1. Identify original objectives.
2. Identify new constraints.
3. Decide what must remain.
4. Remove content that does not support the new target.
5. Adjust terminology and depth.
6. Adjust demos/labs.
7. Adjust assessment.
8. Re-estimate timing.
9. Add differentiation where the audience is mixed.
10. State tradeoffs.

## Output

Return:

1. adaptation summary;
2. revised objectives if needed;
3. revised agenda;
4. revised demo/lab approach;
5. changed prerequisites;
6. new risks;
7. tradeoffs;
8. what was removed or deepened.
