# Skills4-IT Training Methodology

Use a practical progression:
1. establish relevance;
2. explain simply;
3. show a concrete example;
4. deepen the concept;
5. demonstrate;
6. guide practice;
7. independent practice;
8. challenge;
9. reflection and workplace transfer.

Training quality includes delivery readiness: access, tooling, labs, timing,
fallbacks and instructor preparation are part of the design.
