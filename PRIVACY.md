# Privacy Policy

AgentKit is an open-source collection of Codex plugins and skills maintained by Skills4-IT.

AgentKit itself does not operate a hosted service or intentionally collect personal data. Individual plugins may interact with tools, repositories, APIs, MCP servers or third-party services selected by the user. Data handling for those external services is governed by their own policies and by the configuration chosen by the user.

Do not place secrets, credentials or sensitive personal data in AgentKit files. Use environment variables, secret stores and least-privilege credentials for integrations.

For questions about this repository, use the GitHub repository issue tracker.
