# Skills4-IT Training Methodology

## Design principles

1. Start simple, then deepen.
2. Use concrete real-world examples before abstraction.
3. Make learning work-relevant.
4. Technical training should be hands-on-heavy.
5. End-user training should prioritize adoption, confidence and productivity.
6. Use interaction questions, mini-challenges and reflection.
7. Labs need explicit prerequisites, environment checks, checkpoints and troubleshooting.
8. Training quality includes delivery readiness, not only content quality.
9. Always consider access, licenses, permissions, MFA, network restrictions and fallbacks.
10. Every learning objective must be practiced or assessed.

## Preferred learning progression

```text
hook / relevance
→ explanation
→ example
→ demo
→ guided practice
→ independent practice
→ challenge
→ reflection
```

## Real delivery constraints matter

Design for environments where:

- cloud access may be restricted;
- participants may have mismatched permissions;
- local tooling may be blocked;
- trials may expire;
- MFA may interrupt;
- enterprise proxies may interfere;
- not every participant starts at the same level.

A good training design includes fallback paths before delivery day.
